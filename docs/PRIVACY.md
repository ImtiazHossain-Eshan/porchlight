## The short version

- There is **no account**. Porchlight never asks for a name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
- Your game is **kept on your phone**, and in your Android backup if backup is on.
- **Ads only play when you tap to watch one.** There are no banners and no ads between rounds.
- For anyone under 13, ads are **child-directed**: never personalised and rated G at most.
- **Purchases go through Google Play.** Porchlight never sees card or bank details.

## What Porchlight keeps on the phone

- Your progress: levels, stars, badges, lead moths, settings, and the daily porch's record.
- Your Fireflies: the balance, how many were earned and spent, the last 200 changes to them (kept
  for support), and the receipts of anything bought (so each purchase is handed over exactly once).
- The year of birth given on the first screen. It is used only to choose what kind of ad may be shown.
- How many ads were watched today, so the daily limits work.

This stays in the app's private storage. If Android backup is on, Android copies it to the backup of
the Google account on the phone, so a reinstall or a new phone can carry on. Porchlight has no server
and does not receive any of it.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

Porchlight shows **rewarded ads only, and only when the player taps a button** that says what it
gives (for example "Watch for 10 Fireflies"). No ad is requested at all until a year of birth has
been given.

Ads come from Google AdMob, which receives information from the phone to show ads, count them and
prevent fraud. Depending on the player's age and where they are, this may include the advertising
ID, IP address, device and app information, and how the ad was interacted with. What Porchlight asks
of Google depends on the year of birth:

| Age (counted from the youngest the player could be) | How ads are requested |
|---|---|
| Under 13 | Child-directed treatment (COPPA), under the age of consent (GDPR), content rated **G** at most, **never personalised**. No consent form is shown. |
| 13 to 15 | Under the age of consent, content rated **PG** at most, **never personalised**. |
| 16 and over | Content rated **PG** at most. In the EEA and the UK, Google's consent form asks before any personalised ad; the choice can be changed any time from the Privacy screen. Everywhere else ads are **non-personalised**. |

Google's own privacy policy explains how Google uses this information:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Purchases (Google Play)

Fireflies packs, the starter pack and Supporter are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play
handles the payment; Porchlight receives only Google Play's receipt for the purchase (a purchase
token), which it keeps on the phone so the purchase is granted exactly once and can be restored.
A grown-up check stands in front of every purchase screen.

## Children

Porchlight is made for everyone, including children under 13. For them it collects nothing itself,
shows only child-directed ads that are never personalised, and keeps purchases behind a grown-up
check. Parents can see what has been bought in Settings ("For parents") and in the Google Play
order history, and can change the year of birth from the Privacy screen (behind the grown-up check).

## Your choices

- **Don't want ads?** Never tap a "Watch" button; the game is complete without them.
- **Personalised ads (16+, EEA/UK):** change your choice under Settings > Privacy > Privacy choices for ads.
- **Delete everything:** uninstall Porchlight, or clear its storage in Android's app settings. To
  remove the backup too, delete it from the Google account's backups.
